## Principal conclusions

- Opening claim: use **bootstrap-supported regional association reversal** only where both regional intervals exclude 0.5 on opposite sides; point-direction reversal alone is insufficient.
- Evia: `evia_2021_extended` is the canonical, high-prevalence different-regime sensitivity/secondary control, not an equal-prevalence primary validation region.
- Marginal AoA: no component alone reproduced the 12-direction transfer ranking; marginal diagnostics alone were insufficient.
- Window closure: exact natural deltas and interval support are in the final table; negative Brier delta means lower/better.
- Few-shot: supervised label-aware block-selection sensitivity; selection intervals are not confidence intervals.
- Decomposition: negative recovery is retained as `adapted − raw`.
- Muğla: within-region performance is compatible with a volume contribution, but its transfer role is not explained by N alone.
- CORAL: Bejís↔Muğla thermal behaviour is preserved across the λ grid; no “best” or “optimal” λ is claimed.
